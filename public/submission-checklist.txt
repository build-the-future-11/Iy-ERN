IYERN author preparation checklist

Choose your route:
- Journal: a developed manuscript for editorial assessment and peer review.
- Forum: a working paper or revision for moderation and discussion.

Prepare one readable manuscript link with access granted to reviewers.
Include the title, abstract, authors and contributions, version and date.
In the manuscript, explain the question, prior work, method, evidence and limitations.
State data/code availability and any restrictions.
Disclose prior versions, concurrent submissions, funding, conflicts, ethics and AI assistance.
Obtain all authors' approval and permission to share the materials.
Keep identifiable participant data, confidential financial data and identity documents out of the submission.
State what feedback or mentorship would help; this is separate from acceptance.
